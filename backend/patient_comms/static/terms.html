SMS Messaging Terms of Service

Last updated: July 20, 2026

These terms govern the patient text-messaging (SMS) program operated by AI Layer For Access (“we,” “us”), which helps patients complete referrals to social-service resources such as transportation, food, housing, and utility assistance.

Program description & consent

After a member of your care team creates a referral for you, we send an initial consent message. You are enrolled only after you reply YES. By enrolling you agree to receive SMS messages about that referral, including a booking confirmation, an appointment reminder, and a follow-up confirming whether you were able to use the service. We do not send marketing messages.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the status of your referral — typically a small number of messages per referral.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier’s standard messaging and data charges may apply to messages exchanged with us.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe; you will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP, or contact aneesh.swamy@gmail.com or (650) 695-8462, for assistance.

Privacy
