SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This policy describes how AI Layer For Access (“we,” “us”) handles information in connection with our patient text-messaging (SMS) program, which helps patients complete referrals to social-service resources (such as transportation, food, housing, and utility assistance).

Consent to receive messages

You will only receive text messages from us after a member of your care team creates a referral for you and you reply YES to our initial consent message. By replying YES, you agree to receive SMS messages related to that referral, including a booking confirmation, an appointment reminder, and a follow-up to confirm you were able to use the service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the status of your referral. You can typically expect a small number of messages per referral (for example: a consent request, a booking confirmation, a reminder, and a follow-up).

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier’s standard messaging and data charges may apply to messages you send to or receive from us.

Opting out and getting help

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts. Reply START to opt back in. For help, reply HELP or contact us at aneesh.swamy@gmail.com or (650) 695-8462.

How we use and share your mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with any third party for their own marketing. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

We use your mobile number solely to operate this messaging program — to obtain your consent, share the details of a booked service, remind you about it, and confirm whether you were able to use it. We may share information only with service providers who help us deliver these messages (such as our SMS provider), and only as needed to operate the program.

Information we collect

In connection with this program we collect your name, mobile phone number, the service type and organization you were referred to, and the content and timing of the messages exchanged. We retain this information only as long as needed to support your referral and as required by applicable law.

Contact
